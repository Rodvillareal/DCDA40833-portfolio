Tableau Visualization

Reflection

Data Preparation

I ended up getting my data set from Kaggle since I have used it before in a class with Professor Rode and I am comfortable with it. That was the same reason that I used instant data scrapper and it was already on my browser. There wasn’t much work that I needed to do before putting it into Tableau. I did have to clean up some of the columns about the factors in the data, but it didn’t take more than five minutes.

Design Choices

I went with a bar graph as I think that it showed the social media platforms and the messages that were received. I also decided to go with different colors to tell the platforms away from each other and make them stand out more. I thought that would do a good job to make them stand out and keep the viewer interested in the graph. I chose to highlight the messages received on different platforms from both genders. I did this to show the traffic that all of the different platforms get while also showing the differences in traffic and messages that each gender gets.

Expected Patterns

The patterns seemed pretty standard to me with each of the platforms receiving a solid amount of messages received. I also wasn’t too surprised to see that females received higher messages on some platforms compared to males. Looking at Instagram backs this up with females receiving 33 messages compared to 10 messages for males. This backs up what I expected and I am not surprised. The platforms that received the most attention from males and females were pretty different with Snapchat for females and Twitter/X for males. I am not surprised to see this as through my knowledge of social media, females use Snapchat more than men and are likely to receive more messages. I personally used Twitter and most of the people that I see on it are men, so that could be a reason for it being higher compared to females. Sports Twitter is something that is big and that leads a lot of males spending a lot of time on Twitter and sending messages to each other, driving up the numbers.

Surprises/Outliers

The biggest surprise to me was Facebook as males received more votes than females with 13 to 5. I thought that females would receive more messages than males when it came to most platforms.

Questions

What is the number of total men on Facebook compared to women? Do men spend more time on Facebook compared to women? Do men send more messages compared to women?

Tool Evaluation

The tool does a great job at bringing data and being able to visualize the metrics comparing them to each other. I found it easier than using excel as it gives you a lot of options to be able to visualize the results. I like how you bring the CSV file easily and then you can create a sheet or two to show the information in a way to catch attention and help people understand it. I didn’t really have any limitations when I was working on this.
