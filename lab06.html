Design Inspiration

The inspiration that I use for my map was based off the book Red Rising. They have different colors that correspond to the Hierarchy of society and how much power a person has based on that. I decided to make the larger areas like the sky, water and the land off of the more common colors like red, brown and pink who are the bottom of society in the books. For colors that are higher in society they are smaller areas like point of interest labels being gold and place labels being silver.

Cartographic Challenges

The main thing that I had a challenge with was trying to make sure that the colors that I chose worked well with the elements that I assigned them to and didn’t blend into each other too much. When I chose the features, it came to which ones that I thought would fit the map and the area without having the colors clash too much. I worked through a couple of features and looked into the ones that would make the most sense and stand out.

Technical Challenges

I did use claude sonnet 4.5 and 4.6 to help me complete this. I did run into an issue when I asked it to read the CSV and it only came up with six locations instead of the ten that I came up with. At first I copied and pasted the missing locations in and to add them, however the information was a lot and slowed down 4.5. So I switched to 4.6 and asked to specifically look for the other locations that it could not find the first time. This led to it being able to read them and adding them into the map. Other than that, there were not any serious issues.
