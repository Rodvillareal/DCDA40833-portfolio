Tool 1: Gemini

Capabilities

The first AI that I used was Google Gemini and it was successful for the most part. I used Google Gemini as the text/chat assistant for the assignment. I gave it some easy questions to start like to give me some writing advice and how to write good character profiles to build characters. It was able to solve this easily enough. It was also good with things that had recently happened. I asked who was going to be in the Superbowl and I thought that this might be tough for it since the games happened yesterday. It got it right naming the Patriots and the Seahawks and it gave me history on the two of them too. It told me about their previous matchup in the Superbowl and the important stats from that game.

Appropriate Use

I was pretty impressed with what it knew about recent events as I have used other AIs and they have not known that. Something that it did struggle with was files from my desktop. I uploaded a file from my desktop about some characters that I had written for a creative writing class. I told it to give me a day in the life for them and it did it for a few characters before stopping. I had to tell the AI to do it for all of the characters and it did. So it worked fine, but it took extra prodding from me to get it to perform the task. That was the most trouble that I had from Gemini as it worked quite well for me.

Ethical Considerations

I did not really see any ethical issue with Gemini from my use of it and I think that really just comes down to what people are using it for and that they need to check what the AI says before they use it for their task.
